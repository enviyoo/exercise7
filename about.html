my story

i’ve been drawing since i was 4. growing up, i watched a lot of cartoons, especially chinese 动画 (donghua) and japanese anime, and those sparkly big eyes and detailed designs basically sparked my love for anything cute!

i got my first drawing tablet at 11 and have mostly worked digitally since, even though i studied traditional art in a fine arts high school. now i’m studying interaction design in university!
